# Keys and panels

*Everything the keyboard does. Press `?` in the app for the list — or tap the **?** button in
the title bar, which is the way in on a phone or tablet where there is no key to press.*

**The list is also the menu.** Every row whose shortcut can be fired on its own is a
button: tap it and it does what the key does, so the panels are all reachable without a
keyboard. The rows that stay plain text are the ones that need a gesture, such as a drag or
a scroll.

The top bar holds your keycard, the scene switcher and the source picker. Hover over each
control for a tooltip saying what it does.

## The camera

The office is seen from one fixed angle, and the camera never turns. It moves between two
distances: one room, and the whole floor.

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Scroll**, or **+** and **−** | Zoom in on a room, or out to the floor. The same buttons are at the bottom right |
| **Click a room** | Zoom into that room |
| **Drag** | Look around |
| **Click an agent** | Their detail panel: status, current job, and the last ten jobs |

## The keys

| Key | Does |
| --- | --- |
| `?` | The shortcuts panel. |
| `Esc` | Close the open panel, or drop the selected agent. |
| `+` and `-` | Zoom in on a room, or out to the floor. |
| `A` | Show or hide the agents roster. |
| `D` | **Datasources**: which harnesses fill this office. It can hold several at once. |
| `T` | Post a job into the office. Only offered with Test Data. |
| `Y` | Post a job that comes with a checklist. Only with Test Data too. |
| `G` | The activity log: every event, as text. |
| `V` | The developer panel. |

**`T` and `Y` disappear** when the office's source cannot invent work. Test Data can, a live
harness cannot, and inventing jobs on a live feed would be the office telling you a lie
about your agents.

## Every panel also closes with a click

Each one carries an **×** in its own top-right corner. A key is a shortcut, not the only
door: if you opened a panel by clicking something, you should not have to learn a key to put
it away again.

## The room comes back the way you left it

Put the agents roster away and it stays away, and the same goes for the developer panel. It
is remembered **by this browser**, not by the office: send somebody your keycard and they get
the room, not your screen. If your browser will not keep anything, as in a private window,
the panels simply open the way they always did.

An agent's **detail panel** is not remembered, as it needs an agent, so it waits for you to
pick one.
