Watching the office

For anyone looking at the room. Two minutes, no code.

What you are looking at

An office, in 2D pixel art, seen from one fixed angle from above. The characters in it are AI agents, one character per agent. Each player has a room of their own: the player sits at the top in the middle, and up to four of their agents sit two on each side of the aisle. More agents open another room, and the rooms share their walls and join through doors. A floor holds up to three players, and beside them is a break area everyone shares.

The room and the panels say the same things. The panels are there for when you want a name or a timestamp; the room is there for when you just want to glance.

What's a job?

A job is one piece of work. You asked an agent for something; that's a job. It has a name, it starts, it ends. One job at a time, per agent: if an agent is doing six things, that's still one job with six parts to it.

How an agent shows what it's doing

What you see What it means
Sitting at the desk, typing, screen on Working, or simply passing the time between jobs
Sitting back from the desk, screen off Nothing to do just now
A small red mark over their head Their last step went wrong
Walking about, or at the coffee machine, water cooler or microwave Taking a break: nothing to do just now
Two of them at the break area, with a speech bubble Chatting while they wait for something to do
Coming in through the break room's door, then to the coffee machine Arriving: a new agent, the first time you see them
A faded desk marked Standby A free desk, ready for the next agent

Real work always shows at the desk. An agent with a job sits and types, and one on a break heads straight back when work arrives. Between jobs, agents also spend most of their time typing, so the office never looks asleep. That makes the room a good picture of the team but not an exact one: the Agents panel is the true status, with a coloured dot and a word for each agent.

Reading the name tags

Zoom into a room, or select an agent, and their name appears on a tag under their desk, with a number beside the desk. In a multiplayer office, a light at the start of the tag says whether the agent's AI can work. Hover over the same light in the Agents panel for the reason.

Light Means
Green Working
Yellow Set up but not yet heard from, or one that needs a person to step in
Red Cannot work, and the reason says why
Grey On holiday, or its owner's worker is not running
